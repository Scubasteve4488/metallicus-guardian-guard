Corrections

If something here is wrong

A project whose whole claim is “we tell you what we could not verify” has to be correctable in public. This is how.

The process

  1. You identify a specific claim. Not “this page is wrong” — the sentence, the figure, or the label you are challenging. A specific claim can be checked; a general complaint cannot.
  2. The receipt or page version and the challenge are recorded. What was said, where, when, and what you say is wrong with it.
  3. The original source and retrieval time are rechecked. Against the primary source, not against our own notes about it.
  4. New evidence is separated from what existed at delivery time. A claim that was right when written and is now stale is a different failure from a claim that was never supported, and the two get different answers.
  5. The result is marked: corrected, clarified, upheld, or could not resolve.
  6. The change is visible. A public sample or report shows a correction marker and its history. The conclusion does not quietly change underneath a reader who already acted on it.

What counts as a correction

Yes

A factual error. A stale figure presented as current. A claim with no source. A label misused — something marked verified that was not read from a primary source, or a failed read printed as a zero. A capability described as live when it is not. An image or description that breaks a community project’s own canon.

Not really

Disagreement with a judgement that the evidence supports. A request to remove an accurate attention item. A request to add a claim that nothing supports. Those get an answer, but they are not corrections — and disagreement goes back to the primary evidence, not to who said it louder.

How to raise one

Through any route on the contact page. Include the page or receipt, the exact claim, and what you believe is correct — with a source if you have one.

If the claim is about you or your project — an account, a token, a collection, a community page — say so. Claims about identifiable people and projects get checked first, not last.
