If something here is wrong

A project whose whole pitch is "we tell you what we couldn't check" has to be correctable in public.

⚠
Draft, like the privacy and terms pages. The process below is what actually happens.

21 September 2026

How it works

  1. You point at the exact claim. The sentence or the number, not "this page is wrong". A specific claim can be checked.
  2. We write down what was said, where and when, and what you say is wrong with it.
  3. We recheck it at the source — the real one, not our own notes about it.
  4. We separate new facts from old mistakes. A claim that was right then and is stale now is a different problem from one that was never true.
  5. We mark the result: corrected, clarified, upheld, or couldn't resolve.
  6. The change is visible. The answer doesn't quietly shift under someone who already acted on it.

What counts

Yes

A wrong fact. An old number shown as current. A claim with no source. A failed check printed as a zero. Something listed as working that isn't. Art that breaks a project's own canon.

Not really

Disagreeing with a judgement the evidence supports. Asking us to remove something accurate. Asking us to add something nothing backs up.

You'll still get an answer. We just go back to the evidence, not to who argued hardest.

How to raise one

Any route on the contact page. Tell us the page, the exact claim, and what you think is right.

If it's about you or your project, say so. Those get looked at first.
