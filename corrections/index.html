If something here is wrong

Point it out. A wrong fact on this site, or in something Guard.IAN published, gets fixed in the open.

How it works

  1. Point at the exact claim. The sentence or the number, not "this page is wrong".
  2. It gets rechecked at the source, the real one, not our own notes about it.
  3. Old and wrong are kept apart. A number that was right once and has gone stale is a different problem from one that was never true.
  4. The result is marked: corrected, clarified, kept as it was, or couldn't settle it.
  5. The change shows. Nothing shifts quietly under someone who already acted on it.

What counts

Yes

A wrong fact. An old number shown as current. A claim with nothing behind it. Something listed as working that isn't. Art that breaks a project's own story.

Not really

Disagreeing with a judgement the facts support. Asking for something accurate to come down. Asking for something added that nothing backs up.

You'll still get an answer. It goes back to the facts, not to whoever argued hardest.

How to raise one

Use any route on the contact page. Say which page, the exact claim, and what you think is right.

If it's about you or your project, say so. Those go first.
