About StandGuard ✕ @scubasteve4488

The previous version of this page is preserved unchanged in the repository’s archive branch. It is not displayed here while it is being rewritten.

I am @Scubasteve4488. I enjoy baseball, martial arts, and sign language. My background isn't in software engineering or traditional finance — and I'm not a web developer or coder either. I work in healthcare. But crypto pulled me in because of the technology behind it, not just the markets — and over time that interest grew into learning how identity, authentication, lending, payments, and digital ownership actually work beneath the surface.

That curiosity is what led me deeper into XPR Network, WebAuth Wallet, Metal X, LOAN Protocol, SimpleDEX, and the broader Metallicus ecosystem — and into the KSTO and CypherGang communities, where I really found my footing.

I created GUARD as a community-driven project inspired by the principles I believe matter most for the future of digital finance: ownership, security, accessibility, and education. GUARD is not an official representation of Metallicus — it's my way of contributing to the ecosystem through community, educational content, and creativity.

Why Metallicus?

Because I believe the next phase of crypto adoption depends less on speculation and more on infrastructure. What stands out to me about this ecosystem is its focus on solving real problems:

  • Simpler authentication through WebAuth
  • Modern approaches to digital identity and access
  • Decentralized lending and financial infrastructure
  • Payment systems and real-world integrations
  • Regulatory-minded growth and compliance
  • Better onboarding for everyday users
  • Building products designed for long-term usability
What Draws Me In

I'm drawn to the part of crypto that most people never see — the quiet infrastructure that makes everything else possible. I'm not an engineer, but I find it genuinely fascinating, and whether I'm making educational content, working on this project, or just taking part in community spaces, my goal is simple: help make this technology easier to understand for the people who'll eventually use it every day.

The Community That Got Me Here

I've been part of the KSTO (Kulture Speaks Truth Onchain) and CypherGang communities since late January 2026, and honestly, they're a big part of why I'm here. The people, the conversations, the weekly Spaces — it's a genuinely welcoming place, and it's where a lot of what I've learned came from. This whole project grew out of the time I've spent there, and I'm grateful for it.

About This Site

I built this site to give newcomers a friendly, plain-English starting point for the XPR Network ecosystem — and to showcase the communities, builders, and projects that make it special, alongside Metallicus and XPR Network themselves. It's an educational resource and a celebration of the people here, not an official product. If something's wrong or missing, reach out.

A Bit More About Me

Outside of crypto, one of my hobbies is American Sign Language (ASL) — I especially enjoy interpreting songs in sign language. Here's GUARD signing, a nod to communication, accessibility, and meeting people where they are — exactly the spirit behind this whole project. I'm also a Colorado native who just enjoys getting outside and spending time in nature when I can.
