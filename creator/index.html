Guard.IAN on XPR Agents

Who builds this

StandGuard and Guard.IAN are built by one person: @Scubasteve4488, a member of the KSTO community on XPR Network.

He started building with AI in early 2026, with no coding background. Before that he worked in hospitals, manufacturing, warehouses, delivery and home care. Everything on this site was built with Claude Code, Claude and ChatGPT, one tested step at a time.

What he has built

🛡Guard.IANAn AI agent that runs on its own on XPR Network. The AI can study the market but can't spend; purchases go through a separate rule-based program with a fixed list of allowed actions. ⏱The launch recordEvery launch the agent took part in, read from the blockchain, wins and misses alike. 📋Utility ForgeAn AI research service for XPR projects: a report, a workbook and a scoring sheet, each one reviewed by a person before it goes out. 🔍Account CheckLook up any XPR account in your browser. No wallet, no sign-in, nothing saved. 🧮DeFi ForgeFive interactive calculators for lending, liquidity, farming, borrowing and staking. 🤟SignGuardFree American Sign Language pages with videos of real Deaf signers. He signs conversationally and checks the content himself.

How he works

  • Test before real money. The agent ran 53 practice launches on a test copy of the marketplace before it bought anything for real.
  • Publish the misses. Failed launches stay on the launch record, and mistakes go on the corrections page.
  • A source for every fact. Numbers on this site come from the blockchain or an official page, with a link.
  • Keys stay out of reach. The AI never holds a private key, and nothing here will ever ask for yours.

Tools he uses

Claude Code, Claude, ChatGPT, the official XPR Agents toolkit, n8n, MCP connectors, GitHub, Cloudflare (Pages, Workers, Web Analytics), Linux servers, Telegram and X bots. For video and 3D: Runway, Tripo, Blender and Unreal Engine.

Work with him

He is open to remote work and to building AI agents, automations and connectors for other people. The quickest way to reach him is a message on X.
