Limits that apply to every report

  • No safety verdict. Guard.IAN does not and will not say that an account, token, creator, agent, project or transaction is safe. A report describes evidence, not trustworthiness.
  • No guarantee of completeness. Third-party RPC nodes, indexers and APIs can be incomplete, capped, stale or simply wrong. A report states what it could not establish; it cannot state what it did not know to look for.
  • No price prediction. Ever, in any form, including implied.
  • No accusation beyond reproducible evidence, and no verdict about a named natural person.
  • Accurate as of its timestamp. Chain state changes. Every report carries retrieval times for exactly this reason.
  • Not an audit. Not a smart-contract audit, not a security audit, not a legal review, not certification, and not an endorsement.

Tools on this site

The account check and the DeFi Forge are provided as-is for education. The Forge is a simulator: every figure it shows comes from numbers you typed about example tokens that do not exist. It does not model real rates, real risk, impermanent loss in the real world, smart-contract failure, or liquidation in live conditions.

This site has no wallet connection and no signing path. It will never ask you to approve a transaction.
