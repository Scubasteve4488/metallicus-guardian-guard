KSTO’s mascot

Snippy

Snippy is the mascot of the KSTO community and one of the most recognised characters across the XPR Network ecosystem — always present, always building, always snipping.

The character

Snippy belongs to KSTO. Snippy is its mascot and its mascot token — a community identity, not a StandGuard one, and not something GUARD speaks for.

Design canon

Snippy’s design is fixed wherever it appears, and this is the reference:

  • An orange-and-white fluffy fox or dog-like character.
  • Big, expressive eyes.
  • A cheerful face.
  • A purple headband labelled SNIPPY.
  • A purple bandana and accessories.

Anything that departs from that is not Snippy. If an image on this site ever does, it is a defect — please report it through corrections.

Where to find Snippy

Snippy NFTs have been distributed through the wider XPR NFT tooling, including nftpacks.io. KSTO’s own channels are the authority on what is currently available — not this page.
