Snippy

KSTO's mascot, and one of the best-known characters on XPR Network.

Whose it is

Snippy belongs to KSTO. It's their mascot and their token.

Not ours, and we don't speak for it.

How Snippy looks

The design is fixed. This is the reference:

  • Orange and white, fluffy, fox or dog-like.
  • Big expressive eyes.
  • A cheerful face.
  • A purple headband that says SNIPPY.
  • A purple bandana.

Anything different isn't Snippy. If an image here gets it wrong, that's a fault — tell us.
