The Community

KSTO (Kulture Speaks Truth Onchain) is one of the most active and passionate communities in the XPR Network ecosystem. A gathering place for builders, educators, traders, and holders who believe in the mission of XPR Network. Every Wednesday at 8 PM EST, the community comes together on X Spaces to discuss Metallicus updates, WebAuth wallet news, XPR Network developments, and the future of the ecosystem.

✈ KSTO Telegram ✕ @Keep_Snipping

🦊 Snippy — The KSTO Mascot

Snippy is the beloved fox mascot of the KSTO community and one of the most recognized personalities across the entire XPR Network ecosystem. Snippy represents the spirit of the community — always present, always building, always snipping. Snippy NFTs are available on nftpacks.io and are a badge of honor in the KSTO family.

🎁 nftpacks.io

🎨 Funkyverse

Funkyverse is a creative sub-project living within the KSTO ecosystem. Funky NFTs are part of the XPR Network NFT family, available on nftpacks.io alongside Snippy NFTs. Follow Funkyverse on X for creative community content and updates.

✕ @funkyfVerse 🎁 nftpacks.io

🎙 KSTO Spaces — Every Wednesday at 8 PM EST

Join the KSTO community every Wednesday at 8 PM EST on X Spaces. Topics include Metallicus updates, XPR Network news, WebAuth tips, NFT projects, community highlights, and open discussion. This is where the community comes alive — and where Guardians stand together.
