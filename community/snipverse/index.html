SNIPVERSE

Post, engage, earn SNIPS.

What it is

A Web3 social network built on XPR Network by its founder, Jervis Dent, for crypto people, NFT holders and projects. It sits close to KSTO — ksto.world says its work is built for the Snipverse and Snippy community.

✦ snipverse.com ✕ @snipverse

How it works

Sign in, post, tip, stake

Pick a step.

🔑 WebAuth login

Sign in with your WebAuth wallet. No passwords.

💰 Earn SNIPS

For posting, engaging and adding good content.

💸 Tip anyone

In SNIPS, XPR, DOGE, BTC and other coins.

📈 Stake for more

Staking SNIPS moves you up the membership levels.

🌐 No fees: built on XPR Network, so transfers carry no gas fee.🤖 Bot-free: WebAuth identity checks keep out fake accounts and bots.

💎 SNIPS

Snipverse's own token, 100 billion max supply. Earned by posting and engaging; staked for higher membership levels. It trades on Metal X and KuCoin.
