Social Media That Pays You

Snipverse is a Web3 social media platform built natively on XPR Network by founder Jervis Dent. Designed for crypto enthusiasts, NFT holders, and blockchain projects. Post, engage, and earn SNIPS tokens for every contribution. No passwords — sign in with your WebAuth wallet. Your XPR @username is your Snipverse identity. Snipverse 2.0 launched March 24, 2025 with a completely rebuilt experience.

💎 SNIPS Token

SNIPS (Snipcoins) is the native utility token of Snipverse with a 100 billion max supply. Earn SNIPS by posting and engaging. Stake SNIPS to unlock premium membership levels with higher rewards. Available on Metal X and KuCoin.

Membership Levels

Snipverse uses a staking-based membership system. The more SNIPS you stake, the higher your tier — unlocking greater earning potential, exclusive platform features, and community recognition.
