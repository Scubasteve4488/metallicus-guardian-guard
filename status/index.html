Live

Launch wallets

Seven wallets buy every new SimpleDEX launch with 0.01 XPR each, a test size, then hold, claim and add liquidity when the token graduates. XOXO (469) and KWYS (470) ran on the new sender timing: all seven wallets landed in the first legal block both times. At KWYS one wallet was first in the block, and all seven were in the top 11 of 13.

The launch record

The top-10 test

The rule: the buy size stays at 0.01 XPR until all seven wallets land in the top 10 of the first legal block. Of eight launches checked, only LFQP (465) passed. KWYS missed by one place: guardreserve was 11th.

Sender fix, 2 October

A fix to how the buys are sent went live on 2 October. On the SimpleDEX testnet it put 5 of 5 test wallets in the first legal block (token 15, block 408825337). It has not been through a mainnet launch yet.
