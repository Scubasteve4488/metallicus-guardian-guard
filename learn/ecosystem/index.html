Ecosystem

The Metallicus Ecosystem

A short map of the tools and tokens around XPR Network. Each card links to the project's own site. Go there for the full picture before you act on anything.

⚠
Re-read against the official sites on 27 September 2026. Products change. If a card and the official site disagree, the official site is right.
⚒ Foundation

Metallicus

The company behind XPR Network, Metal Blockchain, Metal L2, WebAuth, Metal X and Metal Pay. It builds payment, identity and digital-dollar infrastructure for banks and for people.

metallicus.com

Metal Blockchain (L0)

A Layer 0 chain for financial institutions: EVM-compatible, with subnets for banks that need their own network.

metalblockchain.org

FedNow

Metallicus is listed in the Federal Reserve's FedNow service explorer, the US instant-payment system. The link goes to that listing.

explore.fednow.org
⚡ Network

XPR Network (L1)

The Layer 1 chain Guard.IAN lives on. No gas fees, fast transfers, readable @usernames, and on-chain identity verification.

xprnetwork.org

Metal L2

Metallicus's Ethereum Layer 2, built on the OP Stack.

metall2.com

WebAuth Wallet

XPR Network's own wallet. Your keys stay on your device, you sign with your face or fingerprint, and setup gives you a 12-word recovery phrase to keep offline.

webauth.com

XPR Staking

Stake XPR short or long term inside WebAuth. Staking lets you vote for block producers and pays rewards that the network sets and can change.

xprnetwork.org

XPR Governance

Block producers are elected by people who stake XPR. Proposals and votes happen on the governance portal.

gov.xprnetwork.org

Grants & bounties

XPR Network runs grants and a bounty program for people building on it. Details are on xprnetwork.org.

xprnetwork.org
🏦 Apps & DeFi

Metal X

Metallicus's exchange: an on-chain order book with market and limit orders, liquidity pools, farms and lending. Tokens are listed by Metal X, not by anyone who wants to.

metalx.com

SimpleDEX

The token launchpad and swap built by ProtonNZ. New tokens start on a bonding curve and move to a pool when they graduate. It's where GUARD launched, and where Guard.IAN buys.

simpledex.fun

BlastPad

Launchpad and staking platform ($BLPAD) — create tokens, run airdrops, and set up staking pools.

blastpad.io

Alcor

An exchange where anyone can list a token, with limit orders, pools and farms.

proton.alcor.exchange

LOAN Protocol

The lending protocol behind Metal X lending, and its LOAN token used for governance.

Learn more

Metal Pay

Metallicus's app for buying crypto with a debit or credit card. Buying XPR there works in the US, Australia and New Zealand.

metalpay.com

Metal Dollar (XMD)

A dollar stablecoin backed by a basket of other stablecoins, such as USDC, PYUSD and USDP. You can mint and redeem it 1:1 against the basket. The Metal DAO reviews what goes in the basket.

metaldollar.com
