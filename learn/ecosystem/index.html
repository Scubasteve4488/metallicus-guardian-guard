The Metallicus Ecosystem

A quick, plain-English map of the tools and tokens. Every card links to the official source — that is always the best place for full detail, and it is the place to check before acting on anything here.

Last verified 2026-08-12; carried forward unchanged on 2026-09-21. The descriptions below were accurate when written. Product names, features and availability change. Treat every line as unverified until you have checked it against the linked official source.
⚒ Foundation

Metallicus

The U.S. fintech (founded 2016) building a regulated Digital Asset Banking Network — the company behind everything here.

metallicus.com

Metal Blockchain (L0)

A BSA-ready Layer 0 built for banks and financial institutions — private subnets, digital identity, compliance-first. Bitcoin Virtual Machine testnet is live on Metal Blockchain.

metalblockchain.org

The Digital Banking Network

Open-source protocol built by Metallicus with built-in digital identity and stablecoin support, designed for secure compliant global transactions.

metallicus.com
🌐

Bonifii

Credit union blockchain network powered by Metal Blockchain, enabling financial institutions to deliver real-time payments and digital asset capabilities.

metallicus.com

FedNow Integration

Metallicus completed the FedNow certification programme and supports live transactions on the US Federal Reserve's instant payment system — bridging crypto and US banking.

metallicus.com

Metal Dollar (XMD)

Metallicus stablecoin pegged to USD, native to XPR Network, used for payments and DeFi within the ecosystem.

Metal Client Services

Enterprise and institutional services built on Metal Blockchain infrastructure — tailored solutions for financial institutions entering the digital asset space.

metallicus.com
⚡ Network

XPR Network (L1)

The Layer 1 blockchain. Zero gas fees, 4,000+ TPS, human-readable @names, and built-in on-chain identity.

xprnetwork.org

Metal L2

An Ethereum Layer 2 built on the OP Stack, connecting Ethereum liquidity to The Digital Banking Network.

metalx.com

WebAuth Wallet

The official non-custodial wallet. Biometric login, no seed phrases, free to create. Your gateway to XPR Network.

webauth.com

WebAuth 3.0 (Coming Soon)

Next-gen WebAuth — passkey support, address book, unified multichain portfolio, AI assistant, and biometric key recovery.

webauth.com

XPR Staking

Stake XPR for short or long terms directly in WebAuth Wallet. Earn rewards and contribute to network security and Block Producer voting.

xprnetwork.org

XPR Governance

Community voting and Block Producer elections govern XPR Network. Stake XPR to vote for validators and participate in on-chain governance.

gov.xprnetwork.org

XPR Grants Fund

Community-funded grant program supporting ecosystem builders and projects. Apply to build on XPR Network with funding from the ecosystem DAO.

xprnetwork.org
🏦 Apps & DeFi

Metal X

The DEX & DeFi hub — gas-free swaps, liquidity pools, farms, and lending (no lockup). Curated tokens.

metalx.com

SimpleDEX

The community token launchpad using bonding curves. Where GUARD was born.

simpledex.fun

BlastPad

Launchpad and staking platform ($BLPAD) — create tokens, run airdrops, and set up staking pools.

blastpad.io

Alcor

The first self-listing order-book DEX on XPR — limit orders, AMM pools, farms, NFTs, and OTC trades.

proton.alcor.exchange

LOAN Protocol

Governance + incentive token for the LOAN Protocol (Metal X Lending). Stake to earn and boost voting power.

Learn more

Metal Pay

The regulated U.S. payments app — the easiest way to buy crypto with fiat and on-ramp into the ecosystem.

metalpay.com

Metal Dollar (XMD)

A non-algorithmic stablecoin basket. Mint/redeem 1:1 with USDC, PYUSD & USDP. Governed by Metal DAO ($XMT).
