How this is made

Real motion, never generated

Every clip is motion capture of a real signer.

Guard.IAN does not generate sign language. He plays captured motion or he says nothing. An AI guessing at a sign teaches people something wrong, and a wrong sign is worse than no sign.

Nothing is watching you

This page has no camera, no account and no tracking of your practice.

If hand-tracking practice is added later, it will run inside your own browser and no video will ever leave your device.
