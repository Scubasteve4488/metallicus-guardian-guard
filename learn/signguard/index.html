SignGuard

Paused. The alphabet is not here yet.

What happened

On 5 October 2026 this page went live showing the ASL alphabet, and said the signs were made by a real person. That was wrong.

The clips came from a public-domain motion-capture archive. The motion in them was captured from a human signer, but what you actually watched was a 3D character rendered in Blender from that data. A 3D model is not a person, and the page should never have claimed otherwise.

It was taken down the same day, within the hour, as soon as it was seen.

ℹ
No one was harmed by this and nothing was collected from anyone — the page had no camera, no account and no tracking. The problem was the claim, and the claim is the thing StandGuard cannot be loose about.

Why it is not simply being fixed

Rewording the page to say “3D character” would have made it accurate and would still have been the wrong page to publish.

Fingerspelling is read off a real hand — its shape, the way it moves between letters, the small differences between one signer and the next. A rendered model smooths those away. Someone learning from it learns a version of the alphabet that no Deaf person actually produces, and will not know that until they try to use it with a human being.

So the rule stands, and it is the same rule Guard.IAN works under everywhere else: show captured footage of a real person, or show nothing. Nothing is better.

What comes back

The same three rules this was always meant to ship under:

  1. Never generated. Guard.IAN does not invent a sign, and does not render one. He plays recorded footage of a human signer or he says nothing.
  2. Nothing leaves your device. If practice with a camera is ever added, the video stays in your own browser.
  3. Corrections are written by people. Any feedback the agent gives is a rule a person wrote and approved, not a model’s guess.

It returns when there is footage of a real signer, correctly licensed, and not before.

This page is also logged on the corrections page, where everything StandGuard has got wrong is kept.
