What is a Stablecoin?

A stablecoin is a crypto token pegged to a stable asset — most commonly the US dollar. 1 USD-stablecoin ≈ $1, always. No price swings: the speed of crypto with the stability of cash.

Meet the Metal Dollar (XMD)

XMD is not backed by one company — it's backed by a basket of established stablecoins: USDC (Circle), PYUSD (PayPal), and USDP (Paxos). A smart contract automatically balances the basket to spread risk. Mint or redeem 1:1 on Metal X anytime. Governed by the Metal DAO community.

Send Money Like a Text

On XPR Network you send to @usernames, not wallet addresses. Type a name, enter an amount, confirm with Face ID or fingerprint. Done — instantly, with zero fees, anywhere in the world.

The Bigger Picture

This isn't theory — real US financial institutions are already building on this infrastructure. Metallicus is FedNow Service-certified (the Federal Reserve's instant payment system) and added Request for Payment certification in March 2026. St. Cloud Financial Credit Union issued Cloud Dollar — the first credit-union-issued stablecoin in the US. The Banking Innovation Program now connects 750+ credit unions, and a 50-credit-union pilot launched with CrossState in May 2026. Banks and credit unions can even add their own stablecoins into the Metal Dollar basket. The rails are being laid right now — quietly.
