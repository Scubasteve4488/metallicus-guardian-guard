New to XPR Network? Six steps.

About fifteen minutes, and free.

1

What XPR Network is

A blockchain where transactions are instant and completely free.

No gas fees, ever. Built for real apps: payments, DeFi, identity and games.

xprnetwork.org → See the tools
2

Get a wallet

WebAuth is the official wallet. You log in with your face or fingerprint.

Go to webauth.com, pick a username, and you're done. It's yours permanently.

You'll get a 12-word recovery phrase during setup. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe.

⚠
Nobody legitimate will ever ask you for that phrase. Not support, not a moderator, not us.
iPhone Android Web browser
3

Get some XPR

XPR is the coin the network runs on. You need a bit to get going.

In the US, Australia or New Zealand? Metal Pay is easiest. Buy with a card, from the same company that built the network.

Elsewhere? KuCoin has the most XPR trading. Gate.io also works, but not for US customers.

What you can do with it

Send money to a @username instantly, for free, anywhere.

Hold XMD, a coin that's always worth about a dollar.

Stake XPR to earn rewards and vote.

New to GUARD

Short version: GUARD is a community meme token and a character. It buys you nothing on this site.

What it is

A meme token on XPR Network, launched on SimpleDEX by a community member.

It trades freely against XPR. Transfers are instant and free.

What it is not

Not official. Not a share of anything.

Not required for any service here, and it never gets you a better report.

⚠
It's a meme token. The price is driven entirely by demand and can drop to nothing. Never spend more than you can afford to lose.
