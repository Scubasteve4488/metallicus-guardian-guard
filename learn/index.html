New to XPR Network?

Six steps, in order

You will be able to send, trade and stake in under fifteen minutes, for free.

1

What XPR Network is

XPR Network is a fast, zero-fee blockchain built for real apps — DeFi, gaming, identity and payments. Transactions settle near-instantly with no gas fees. It runs on Delegated Proof of Stake, meaning elected Block Producers validate all activity.

XPR Network → Explore the ecosystem
2

Create your WebAuth wallet

WebAuth is the official XPR Network wallet. You log in daily with Face ID or fingerprint — no seed phrase needed for normal use. You will get a 12-word recovery phrase during setup; save it somewhere safe in case you ever lose your device. Go to webauth.com to create your account and pick your permanent @username.

⚠
Nobody legitimate will ever ask you for that recovery phrase. Not WebAuth support, not a community moderator, not Guard.IAN, and not this site — no page here has a field for one.
Download on iOS → Download on Android → Use a web browser →
3

Get your first XPR

XPR is the native token of XPR Network — you need some to get started. How you buy it depends on where you are.

Metal Pay — easiest in the US, Australia & New Zealand

Buy XPR directly with a debit or credit card. Built by Metallicus, the same team behind XPR Network.

Buy XPR on Metal Pay →

Outside those regions, KuCoin carries the highest XPR volume; Gate.io is another option but is not available to US customers.

Learn what you can do with it

Send to @usernames with a biometric confirmation, instantly and with zero fees. Hold XMD, a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the US dollar and native to XPR Network. Stake XPR for rewards and to vote for Block Producers.

What GUARD is

A community memecoin on XPR Network, launched on SimpleDEX by a community member and now trading in a live XPR/GUARD liquidity pool. Zero gas fees, instant transfers, fully on-chain.

What GUARD is not

Not an official Metallicus product. Not a claim on revenue, ownership or governance of anything. Not a requirement for any Guard.IAN service, and not something that improves a report you commission.
