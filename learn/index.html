New to XPR Network? Six steps.

About fifteen minutes, and free.

1

What XPR Network is

A blockchain where transfers settle in about half a second.

You don't pay gas fees to send tokens. It's built for payments, trading, identity and apps.

xprnetwork.org → See the tools
2

Get a wallet

WebAuth is XPR Network's own wallet. You sign in with your face or fingerprint.

Get the app or go to webauth.com and pick a username. That username is your account.

Setup shows you a 12-word recovery phrase. Write it down on paper. If you lose your phone, that phrase is the only way back in.

⚠
Nobody legitimate will ever ask you for that phrase. Not support, not a moderator, not us.
iPhone Android Web browser
3

Get some XPR

XPR is the network's own coin. Most apps here expect you to hold a little.

In the US, Australia or New Zealand? Metal Pay sells XPR for a debit or credit card. It's made by Metallicus, the company behind the network.

Elsewhere? XPR is listed on some centralized exchanges, KuCoin among them. Check what's allowed where you live.

Metal Pay → KuCoin →
4

What you can do with it

Send tokens to anyone by @username, without a gas fee.

Hold XMD, the network's dollar stablecoin.

Stake XPR to vote for block producers. Staking pays rewards set by the network, and they change.

More on this →
5

Trade on Metal X

The exchange built for XPR Network, made by Metallicus. Trades carry a fee; moving tokens in and out doesn't cost gas.

New to GUARD

Short version: GUARD is a community meme token and a character. It buys you nothing on this site.

What it is

A community meme token on XPR Network, launched on SimpleDEX.

It trades against XPR like any other SimpleDEX token.

What it is not

Not official. Not a share of anything.

Not required for any service here, and it never gets you a better report.

⚠
It's a meme token. The price is driven entirely by demand and can drop to nothing. Never spend more than you can afford to lose.
