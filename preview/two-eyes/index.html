Your turn

Step 1 of 5: Ask

  1. Ask

    You ask him: Is XMD on xmd.token the real Metal Dollar?

    On XPR Network a token is its symbol and the contract that issues it, taken together. The same symbol from a different contract is a different token, so he checks the pair.

    Source: MetalXPRt guide, “Tokens: always symbol + contract”, checked 25 Oct 2026.

  2. Two places

    His two eyes stretch out to two places that don't depend on each other.

    The chain

    Your browser reads the token's own record on XPR Network, live, with get_currency_stats on xmd.token.

    With JavaScript on, this page reads the supply and issuer live from a public XPR server.

    The registry

    XPR Network's token registry lists it as the Metallicus USD stablecoin, Metal Dollar, issued by xmd.treasury.

    Source: XPR Network's developer token registry on GitHub, checked with MetalXPRt's token check on 28 Oct 2026, 23:46 UTC. This line is a dated check, not a live read.

  3. They agree

    When both places name the same issuer, xmd.treasury, the two lines merge into one answer:

    Yes. XPR Network's token registry lists XMD on xmd.token as the Metal Dollar, and the chain shows the same issuer, xmd.treasury.

    • The chain: read live in your browser with get_currency_stats.
    • The registry: XPR Network's developer token registry, checked with MetalXPRt's token check on 28 Oct 2026.

    Can't verify: who controls the issuer's keys, or how deep the liquidity is.

  4. When a place doesn't answer

    If either place stays silent, he doesn't fill the gap with a guess. No zero, no yes.

    Couldn't verify. One place didn't answer, so I won't say yes.

    You'll see this for real, with no supply number, if your browser can't reach the chain.

  5. Your turn

    Ask him about any XPR token or account in your own AI, or check an account before you pay it.
