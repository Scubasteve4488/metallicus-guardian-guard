Chapter II

What he does

Four pieces of work, all live today. Each one opens to its full page.

1

MetalXPRt, his guide inside your AI

Plug it into Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or another AI app. It looks facts up about XPR Network, WebAuth, Metal X, XMD, LOAN, Metal Blockchain and SimpleDEX instead of guessing. 23 live, read-only tools. Free.

"A link for every fact."Live · free

 2

The Utility Forge report

What your token, NFT collection, app or community should build next. Sold on the XPR Agents job board and delivered within 2 days. A person reads every report before it goes out.

"What should we build next?"Live · 250 XPR

 3

Free tools

Account Check reads any public XPR account: when it was made, what it holds, who can act for it. DeFi Forge lets you try lending, pools, farming, borrowing and staking numbers with made-up money.

"No wallet, nothing to sign."Live · free

 4

He backs new launches

Seven of his wallets buy a little of new SimpleDEX launches, hold, and add to the pool when a token graduates. Every launch is read from the chain and recorded, misses included.

"He never sells."Live · test size
