Account Check

Look up any XPR Network account. See what it holds and what it does, in plain English.

🔒
Nothing connects, nothing is saved. No wallet, no signing, no seed phrase box. Your browser asks public XPR servers directly, the same way a block explorer does. This site has no server, so nothing reaches us.

Letters a–z, digits 1–5, and dots. A leading @ is fine.

What this won't tell you

  • Whether an account is safe. Nothing can. The chain shows what an account did, never who's behind it or what they intend.
  • Whether a token it holds is any good. Anyone can send any token to anyone. Seeing it listed means nothing.
  • Its full history. This shows what's true right now.
  • Anything off this chain. Other blockchains and exchange accounts are invisible here.

Why a failed check says "failed"

If every server refuses, this tool says so. It does not print a zero.

"This account has no liquidity positions" and "we couldn't find out whether it has any" lead to opposite decisions. A tool that blurs those two is worse than no tool.
