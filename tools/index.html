3 — Evidence method

The labels, and what each one commits to

LabelWhat it meansWhat it does not mean
VerifiedRead from a primary source, with the source and retrieval time recorded.That it is still true now, or that the underlying thing is trustworthy.
AttentionSomething in the evidence a reasonable person would want to look at.An accusation, or a claim of wrongdoing.
Could not verifyA check was attempted and did not return a usable answer.That the thing is absent. A failed read is never reported as a zero.
ContradictedA public claim conflicts with the evidence found.That the claimant lied. Stale and wrong are different things.
UnknownNo source was available that could answer it at all.Anything at all. It is the honest empty set.

Rules that hold on every report

  • Missing data is never presented as evidence that nothing happened.
  • Incomplete indexer or RPC coverage is disclosed, not smoothed over.
  • A capped or paginated endpoint never supports a claim of complete history.
  • Conflicting results from different providers are shown as conflicting, not merged into one confident number.
  • Ownership, KYC level, trust score and community membership are never treated as proof of honesty.
  • No verdict is published about a named natural person.
  • No account is named as a bad actor without a block or a trade behind it.
