How reports are built

  • Chain facts come from public XPR Network servers and SimpleDEX's own data, with the date they were read.
  • Anything from the web is linked, so you can open it yourself.
  • If a server doesn't answer, that's written down. It isn't turned into a zero.
  • A trust score or KYC badge isn't treated as proof that anyone is honest.
  • No verdict about a named person. No account called a bad actor without pointing at what it did.
