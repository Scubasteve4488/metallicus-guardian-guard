How we label things

LabelWhat it means
CheckedWe read it from the source and wrote down when.
Worth a lookSomething a sensible person would want to see. Not an accusation.
Couldn't checkWe tried and got nothing back. This never means "it isn't there".
ContradictedA public claim doesn't match the evidence. Could just be out of date.
UnknownNo source could answer it at all.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  • A failed check is never shown as a zero.
  • Missing data never means nothing happened.
  • If a source only gave us part of the picture, we say so.
  • If two sources disagree, you see both.
  • A trust score or a KYC badge is never treated as proof anyone is honest.
  • We never publish a verdict about a named person.
  • We never call an account a bad actor without pointing at what it did.
