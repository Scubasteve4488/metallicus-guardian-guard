What this website counts

No accounts, no cookies, no ads, and no database behind these pages. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we can see which pages are read and how often.

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it doesn't fingerprint visitors and drops the IP address instead of storing it (Cloudflare Web Analytics). We see totals, not people.

One thing this site keeps in your browser: the first-hour checklist remembers which steps you ticked, in your browser's own storage (localStorage, under the name sg-first-hour-v1). It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it; if your browser blocks storage, the ticks last for that visit only.

Two things worth knowing

Chain readings run in your browser

When you press an account-lookup button, your browser asks public XPR servers directly.

Some pages also read the chain on their own, with no button: the live block ticker and the "Ask him" answers on the home page (the ticker every 3 seconds while it is on screen), the Metal Dollar treasury on stablecoins and payments, and the GUARD token supply on GUARD. They go to one of four public XPR servers: proton.eosusa.io, api.protonnz.com, proton.cryptolions.io and api-xprnetwork-main.saltant.io.

Those servers belong to other people. Like any website, they see the request and your IP address, the same as when you use a block explorer.

The result goes to your browser, not to us.

The host sees requests

Cloudflare serves these pages. Like any host, it handles the connection, under its own rules.

The fonts are served from this site too, so loading a page doesn't send your browser to Google or any other font service.

If you buy a report

Reports are bought on the XPR Agents job board. Here's what happens to what you send.

What we getYour WebAuth account name, and your answers to the short order form: your project, its account or token, and what you want looked at.
The form is publicThe job board records your purchase and your form answers on the XPR Network blockchain, where anyone can read them. Don't put anything private in the form.
WhyTo write the report you paid for. Nothing else.
AI and search servicesGuard.IAN is an AI agent. Your project details go to an outside AI provider (OpenAI) and outside web-search services (Brave, Tavily) while the report is written.
ReviewThe operator gets a short Telegram notice (project name, job number) and reads the report before it's delivered.
Your filesKept on our server behind a private link. They open only after you sign in with the WebAuth wallet that paid. Signing in proves the wallet is yours. Nothing is sent or spent, and only a short-lived sign-in pass is kept.
How longUntil you ask for them to be deleted. What the blockchain has already recorded can't be deleted by anyone.
Who sees itYou and the operator. Not other customers, not the public.
Deleting itAsk through the contact page and your files and notes are deleted from our server.

Public accounts are public

Account names, transactions and holdings on XPR Network are visible to anyone. That's how the chain works.

Public doesn't mean fair game. No verdict is ever published about a named person, and nobody is identified from what an address did.

What we will never ask for

⚠
A seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase or wallet password. Not here, not in chat, not ever. No page on this site has a box for one. Anyone asking while claiming to be us isn't us.
