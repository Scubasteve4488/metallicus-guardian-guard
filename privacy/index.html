Privacy

⚠
Updated 27 September 2026 for the first paid service. Written from how things actually work; not reviewed by a lawyer.

First written 21 September 2026.

This website collects nothing

No server. No database. No accounts. No tracking. No ads.

Nothing you do on these pages is recorded by us, because there is nothing here to record it with.

Two things worth knowing anyway

The account lookup runs in your browser

When you press the button, your browser asks public XPR servers directly.

Those servers belong to other people. Like any website, they can see the request and your IP address. Same as using a block explorer.

The result never comes to us. There is no us to send it to.

Our host sees requests

Cloudflare serves these files. Like any host, it handles the connection.

What it logs is covered by its rules, not ours.

Fonts load from Google, so your browser talks to Google too. We plan to stop that.

If you buy a job

Jobs are bought on the XPR Agents job board. Here's what happens to what you send.

What we collectYour WebAuth account name, and your answers to the job's short form: your project, its account or token, what you want looked at.
The form is publicYour purchase and your form answers are recorded on the XPR Network blockchain by the job board, where anyone can read them. Don't put anything private in the form.
WhyTo do the job you asked for. Nothing else.
AI and search servicesGuard.IAN is an AI agent. Your project details are processed by an outside AI provider (OpenAI) and searched on outside web-search services (Brave, Tavily) to write the report.
ReviewThe operator is sent a short notice (project name, job number) on Telegram and reads the report before it is delivered.
Your filesStored on our server behind a private link. They open only after you sign in with the WebAuth wallet that paid. Signing in proves the wallet is yours; nothing is sent or spent, and we keep only a short-lived sign-in pass.
How longUntil you ask us to delete them. What the blockchain already recorded cannot be deleted by anyone.
Who sees itYou and the operator. Not other customers. Not the public.
Deleting itAsk through the contact page, and your files and notes are deleted from our server.

Public accounts are public

Account names, transactions and holdings on XPR Network are visible to anyone. That's how the chain works, and we don't change it.

Public isn't the same as fair game. We never publish a verdict about a named person, and we never decide who someone is from what an address did.

What we will never ask for

⚠
A seed phrase. A private key. A recovery phrase. A wallet password. Not here, not in chat, not ever. No page on this site has a box for one. Anyone asking while claiming to be us is not us.
