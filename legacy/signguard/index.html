Actual signers

Motion capture

In-browser

Hand tracking

21 landmarks per hand at video speed, running locally. Around 98% on fingerspelling in published benchmarks. No upload, no server, no privacy problem.

A rule that lives in the code, not just the pitch: SignGuard never generates a sign with an AI video model. Those produce hands that move without signing anything, and to a Deaf user that is worse than nothing. The player shows real captured motion or it refuses.
Honest limits

What this does and does not do

Works

Letters, numbers, vocabulary

Fingerspelling recognition is a solved problem. It is also where every beginner starts.

Not solved

Conversational signing

Continuous sign recognition is open research. AWS ships something it calls "approximated" ASL. We will not claim otherwise.

Required

Deaf review

A hearing person building ASL tools without Deaf review is how this goes wrong. It is a build requirement before anything ships.

Why XPR Network

Zero gas is not a nice-to-have here

Fifty reps, zero fees

A learner doing fifty practice attempts cannot pay a fee per attempt. On most chains this idea is dead on arrival.

Progress belongs to the learner

Their record sits against their own account, not in our database.

Snipverse pays people to post. Paying people to learn a language is the same mechanic, pointed somewhere that matters.
