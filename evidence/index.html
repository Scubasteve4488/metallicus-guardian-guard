Evidence

See what was checked — not just what the agent concluded.

This page is the standard the work is held to. If a report on this site ever fails one of the rules below, that is a defect and the corrections process applies.

Method

Every answer is built the same way

  1. Verified public facts
  2. Plain-English meaning
  3. Attention items
  4. Could not verify
  5. What would confirm it
  6. Sources and timestamps
  7. Scope and limitations

The fourth and fifth sections are the product. Any wallet tool gives you a green check; the disciplined statement of what could not be established is the part that is actually worth paying for.

The labels

LabelWhat it commits toWhat it does not mean
VerifiedRead from a primary source, with the source and retrieval time recorded.That it is still true now, or that the thing itself is trustworthy.
AttentionSomething a reasonable person would want to look at.An accusation or a claim of wrongdoing.
Could not verifyA check was attempted and did not return a usable answer.That the thing is absent.
ContradictedA public claim conflicts with the evidence found.That anyone lied. Stale and wrong are different.
UnknownNo source could answer it at all.Anything. It is the honest empty set.

Rules that hold on every report

  • A failed read is never rendered as a zero.
  • Missing data is never evidence that nothing happened.
  • Incomplete indexer or RPC coverage is disclosed.
  • A capped or paginated endpoint never supports a claim of complete history.
  • Conflicting provider results are shown as conflicting, not averaged into confidence.
  • Ownership, KYC level, trust score and community membership are never treated as proof of honesty.
  • No verdict is published about a named natural person.
  • No account is named as a bad actor without a block or a trade behind it.
  • A paid report never buys favourable treatment, and holding any token never improves a conclusion.

Receipts

What a published receipt contains

Receipt ID · service or job ID where public · subject · question or scope · created time in UTC · Guard.IAN release · source-lock revisions · evidence sources and retrieval times · verified facts · attention items · could not verify · limitations · correction status · artifact hash where appropriate.

Observations may be immutable. Conclusions stay revocable — no verdict is written to IPFS or to a chain, because a conclusion that cannot be withdrawn is a conclusion that cannot be corrected.
