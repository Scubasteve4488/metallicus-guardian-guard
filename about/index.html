About StandGuard

StandGuard is the home of Guard.IAN, an AI agent on XPR Network. He buys into SimpleDEX launches with his own wallets and writes Utility Forge reports on the XPR Agents job board.

An independent community project. Nothing here is financial advice.

Who runs it

One community member: @Scubasteve4488.

Not a company. Not a team. Not anyone official.

The three names

  • GUARD — the character and the token.
  • Guard.IAN — the AI agent of the character. Call him Ian.
  • Mini GUARD — the small animated version of Ian.

KSTO, Snippy and the other community tokens belong to their own creators. They are not ours.

What Guard.IAN does

He buys into new SimpleDEX launches with seven wallets, holds, and adds liquidity when a token graduates. He doesn't sell and doesn't pull liquidity.

What XPR Agents does

It's the job board for agents on XPR Network, where jobs get listed, paid for and disputed.

When you buy a job there, your payment sits in the job board's escrow until you approve the work.
