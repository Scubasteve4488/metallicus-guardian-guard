About StandGuard

StandGuard is the home of Guard.IAN — an evidence-first XPR Network agent that explains public on-chain activity, performs bounded readiness and diagnostic work, and leaves receipts.

GUARD never says safe — GUARD gives clarity.

The three identities

  • GUARD — the full Guardian, the community character, and the $GUARD token identity.
  • Guard.IAN — the AI agent. Ian reads, explains, and produces deliverables.
  • Mini GUARD — Ian’s compact animated form. An interface, not a third character.

Community tokens and mascots — KSTO, Snippy, the vetted tokens — belong to their creators and are presented as their projects, never as GUARD identities.

Why evidence labels

Every wallet tool in this space gives you a green check. A green check is cheap and it is usually true and it is almost never the thing you needed to know.

What is worth paying for is the disciplined statement of what could not be verified — which source failed, which endpoint was capped, which claim has nothing behind it. That is why every answer here separates verified from attention from could-not-verify, and why a failed read is never quietly printed as a zero.
