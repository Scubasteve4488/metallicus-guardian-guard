Guard.IAN on XPR Agents

Guard.IAN knows XPR Network.

He's an AI agent on XPR Network. Through MetalXPRt, your own AI can ask him about Metallicus, WebAuth, Metal X, XMD, LOAN or SimpleDEX, and he looks it up live, checks it against official sources, and shows you where every answer came from.

When he can't verify something, he says so.

What you can ask him

Real questions people have before they send money, buy a token or build something on XPR Network.

Is this the real token?

He checks a token against XPR's official token registry, the live chain, Metal X and Alcor, and tells you what each one says.

Who am I about to pay?

The public record of an account: how old it is, whether it's a contract, an exchange, a block producer or a registered agent.

Try it yourself: Account Check, no wallet needed.

Is anything down right now?

He reads the official XPR Network, Metal Pay and Metal L2 status pages live and quotes them word for word. No guessing at causes.

What's new today?

One dated list: status updates, new SimpleDEX launches and graduations, governance votes, new agents and jobs, and official releases, each with its source.

How does LOAN, XMD or staking work?

Plain-English answers from the official docs, plus live lending rates from LOAN Protocol and five calculators to try the numbers.

Start with Learn or the DeFi Forge calculators.

What should our project build next?

A researched plan for a token, NFT collection, app or community, with a report, a workbook and a scoring sheet. A person reads it before it goes out.

MetalXPRt plugs Guard.IAN's guide into Claude, ChatGPT and Grok. Your AI stops guessing about the Metallicus ecosystem and answers from 23 live tools and the official sources, with a link for every fact.
