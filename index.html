Guardian Guides

Plain English walkthroughs. Click any guide to expand it.

🔑 How to Set Up Your WebAuth Wallet
  1. Go to webauth.com on your phone or desktop.
  2. Tap Create Account and choose a permanent @username — this is your on-chain identity, pick carefully.
  3. Register your device using Face ID or fingerprint — no seed phrase needed.
  4. Confirm your email for account recovery backup.
  5. Your wallet is live. Your @username is now a permanent XPR Network address you can receive tokens to.
🪪 How to Complete Identity Verification (KYC)
  1. Open the WebAuth app or go to webauth.com and log in.
  2. Tap your profile icon and select Verify Identity.
  3. Choose your country and follow the prompts to upload your government-issued ID.
  4. Take a selfie for the liveness check — the whole process takes about 5 minutes.
  5. Once approved (usually within minutes), you unlock lending, fiat on-ramps, and full DeFi access.
💱 How to Trade on MetalX
  1. Go to metalx.com and connect your WebAuth wallet.
  2. Select the asset you want to trade from and the asset you want to receive.
  3. Enter the amount and review the exchange rate — all trades are zero gas fee.
  4. Confirm in your WebAuth app with your biometric.
  5. Your new balance appears instantly in your wallet.
📈 How to Use Alcor DEX
  1. Go to alcor.exchange and connect your WebAuth wallet.
  2. Browse the market list or search for a trading pair (e.g. GUARD/XPR).
  3. Use Swap for instant market buys, or Limit Order to set your own price.
  4. Confirm each trade via WebAuth biometric — no gas fees.
  5. To earn fees as a liquidity provider, go to Pools, select a pair, deposit both assets.
🔒 How to Stake XPR for Rewards
  1. Open webauth.com or the WebAuth app and log in.
  2. Go to Earn or Stake from your dashboard.
  3. Choose XPR Staking, enter the amount, and select your lock duration.
  4. Confirm with your biometric — rewards begin accumulating immediately.
  5. Claim your staking rewards anytime from the same Earn screen.
⚡ How to Buy GUARD on SimpleDEX
  1. Make sure you have XPR in your wallet — buy via Metal Pay or transfer from an exchange that supports XPR.
  2. Go to simpledex.fun/pool/187 (the GUARD/XPR pool).
  3. Connect your WebAuth wallet using the Connect button.
  4. Enter the amount of XPR you want to swap and confirm the GUARD you'll receive.
  5. Approve the transaction in WebAuth — GUARD lands in your wallet instantly, zero fees.
🚀 How to Use BlastPad Staking Pools
  1. Go to blastpad.io and connect your WebAuth wallet.
  2. Browse the available staking pools — look for Pool 791 to stake GUARD.
  3. Enter the amount you want to stake and review the lock duration (if any).
  4. Approve via WebAuth biometric — your staked balance is visible in the pool immediately.
  5. Return to claim rewards anytime; unstake when your lock period ends.

GUARD Trials — Every 5th barrier in each zone has a 10% chance to drop a shard for that zone's shield. Max 2 random drops per run. Boss barriers always drop their zone's shard.
Wave Survival — Boss kills on waves 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 50 guarantee a shard drop. Regular enemies on waves 1–4 have a 3% chance to drop a Cryptide shard. Max 2 random drops per run.
Forging — Once you collect all 5 shards for a shield, tap the ⚒ FORGE button to trigger the forge ceremony. Forging that shield unlocks it as an alternative rank path — no GUARD balance required.

Shard progress is stored locally in your browser — it will not transfer between devices.

Knowledge Base

GUARD — FAQ

Answers to the questions only this site can answer — about GUARD itself. For ecosystem and DeFi questions, the official help centers (linked below) are the best, most up-to-date source.

What is GUARD?+
GUARD (Metallicus Guardian) is a community meme token on XPR Network launched on SimpleDEX by Tim Rangel. A grassroots community project — not an official Metallicus product. Created to contribute to the ecosystem through community building, education, and advocacy.
Is GUARD an official Metallicus product?+
No. GUARD is entirely community-created and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Metallicus or any ecosystem project. It's a tribute, built by a community member.
How do I buy GUARD?+
You need a WebAuth wallet funded with XPR. Then visit simpledex.fun/token/172 and connect your wallet. The Start Here page walks through wallet setup and funding first.
Can I sell GUARD?+
Yes. GUARD has graduated past its bonding curve to a live XPR/GUARD liquidity pool, so you can trade it any time on SimpleDEX.
What are the risks of holding GUARD?+
GUARD is a speculative meme token. Price is driven entirely by community demand and can rise or fall sharply. Never invest more than you can afford to lose. This is not financial advice.
Why does this site exist?+
To give newcomers a friendly, plain-English starting point for the XPR Network ecosystem — and to point them to the official sources for the full detail. It's a community resource, not an official one.
What is XPR Network?+
XPR Network is a Layer 1 proof-of-stake blockchain built by Metallicus. It offers instant transactions, zero gas fees, on-chain identity verification, and human-readable @usernames. It powers WebAuth Wallet, Metal X, SimpleDEX, and dozens of community projects.
What is a WebAuth Wallet and how do I create one?+
WebAuth Wallet is a free, non-custodial crypto wallet built by Metallicus on XPR Network. It uses biometrics (fingerprint/face ID) instead of passwords — no seed phrase needed. To create one: go to webauth.com, tap Sign Up, choose your @username, and verify with biometrics. It takes under 2 minutes and is completely free.
What are Guardian ranks and how do I unlock them?+
Guardian ranks are based on how much GUARD you hold in your WebAuth wallet. Connect your wallet on the Shields tab to automatically unlock your rank. There are 7 ranks: Cryptide (0), Riftforge (100+), Helion (500+), Nebulon (2,000+), Protonis (10,000+), Keystone (50,000+), and Aegis (200,000+ GUARD).
Where do I buy GUARD?+
GUARD trades on SimpleDEX at simpledex.fun/token/172. You need a WebAuth wallet with some XPR to trade. You can get XPR on Metal Pay, KuCoin, or other exchanges that support XPR Network.
What is Metal Blockchain?+
Metal Blockchain is a Layer 0 blockchain built by Metallicus — a compliance-first, Avalanche-based network designed for banks, financial institutions, and regulated enterprises. It supports private subnets with up to 4,500 TPS, built-in digital identity, and BSA-ready infrastructure. Metal Blockchain is the foundation layer that XPR Network and other Metallicus products build on top of.
What is FedNow and why does it matter?+
FedNow is the US Federal Reserve's instant payment system. Metallicus is the first blockchain-based company integrated with FedNow — meaning XPR Network has a direct bridge to the US banking system for instant, real-world dollar settlement. This is a significant milestone for crypto legitimacy and real-world adoption. See the official listing at the FedNow Explorer.
What is WebAuth 3.0?+
WebAuth 3.0 is the next generation of the WebAuth Wallet — announced and in development. New features include passkey support, a built-in address book, a unified portfolio view across chains, an AI assistant, and biometric key recovery. It will make the already beginner-friendly wallet even more powerful. Visit webauth.com for the latest updates.
How do I stake XPR?+
XPR staking is done directly in your WebAuth Wallet. There are two options: short staking and long staking, each offering different reward rates. Long staking locks your XPR for a longer period in exchange for higher rewards. Your staked XPR also contributes to network resources (CPU and NET) and Block Producer voting power. Visit xprnetwork.org for current staking rates.
Can I build on XPR Network?+
Yes. XPR Network is open source and supports WASM smart contracts. Developers can deploy dApps, tokens, and NFT contracts on the chain. The XPR Grants Fund provides community funding for ecosystem projects — builders can apply for grants to support development. Visit xprnetwork.org to learn more about grants and developer resources.
