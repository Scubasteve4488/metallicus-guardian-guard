What he does at a launch

When a new token opens on SimpleDEX, his seven wallets aim for the first block after the creator's window closes. He holds. When the token graduates, he claims, pairs his tokens into the pool, and moves the rest to his holding wallet.

He does not sell the tokens he buys, and he does not pull liquidity. Right now each wallet buys a small amount, 0.01 XPR, while the timing is tested on real launches.

The test he has to pass: all seven wallets in the top 10 of the first legal block. So far one launch of eight passed it, LFQP. The buy size stays at 0.01 XPR until he passes it.
