The Lore of GUARD

The Metallicus Guardian

Before there were castles, kingdoms, or guardians, there was only the forge.

Deep within the expanding ecosystem of Metallicus, where digital identity, ownership, and decentralized finance converged, fragments of forgotten METAL were gathered by the community itself — builders, creators, traders, educators, meme makers, validators, believers, and everyday users who refused to let decentralization become something only institutions could understand.

From those fragments, GUARD was forged.

Not by kings.
Not by corporations.
Not by decree.
By community.

The forge was fed with METAL itself, while the current of the XPR Network flowed through the armor that would become his body. The result was not merely a warrior — but a protector built for a new frontier.

His armor was forged from scorched METAL alloy, darkened by countless battles against exploitation and deception. Through his veins runs the pulse of the XPR Network — fast, efficient, and always moving.

But his greatest weapon was never his armor.

It was identity.

Upon his arm rests the Guardian Shield — forged from cosmic METAL, it is not a shield of conquest, but of ownership. A reminder that users should control their own access, assets, and identity.

In his grasp is the Proton Key.

No two keys are alike.

Every person who creates a WebAuth wallet forges their own key — unique to them alone. GUARD does not carry one key to rule them all. He carries the symbol of what each individual possesses: access to their own identity, ownership of their own assets, and responsibility for protecting both.

GUARD does not stand watch over wallets.
He stands watch over people.

He watches the gates where newcomers arrive. He walks beside those learning self-custody for the first time. He warns travelers of scammers, phishing attacks, impersonators, fake links, false promises, and those who prey on confusion.

His enemies are not armies.

His enemies are deception, exploitation, centralized dependency, and the belief that ownership should belong to someone else.

Though singular in form, GUARD was never meant to stand alone.

Those who educate.
Those who build.
Those who protect newcomers.
Those who help others learn WebAuth.
Those who support builders and communities.
Those who create.

They stand GUARD beside him.

Every WebAuth wallet becomes a shield.
Every user carries their own key.
Every builder strengthens the walls.

As the Metallicus ecosystem expands — through WebAuth, XPR Network, Metal X, community projects, builders, creators, and the countless people entering for the first time — GUARD remains where he has always stood:

At the gateway.
Watching.
Teaching.
Protecting.
Waiting for the next person to arrive.

Stand GUARD.

GUARD — FAQ

Answers to the questions only this site can answer — about GUARD itself. For ecosystem and DeFi questions, the official help centers (linked below) are the best, most up-to-date source.

What is GUARD?+
GUARD (Metallicus Guardian) is a community meme token on XPR Network launched on SimpleDEX by Tim Rangel. A grassroots community project — not an official Metallicus product. Created to contribute to the ecosystem through community building, education, and advocacy.
Is GUARD an official Metallicus product?+
No. GUARD is entirely community-created and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Metallicus or any ecosystem project. It's a tribute, built by a community member.
How do I buy GUARD?+
You need a WebAuth wallet funded with XPR. Then visit simpledex.fun/token/172 and connect your wallet. The Start Here page walks through wallet setup and funding first.
Can I sell GUARD?+
Yes. GUARD has graduated past its bonding curve to a live XPR/GUARD liquidity pool, so you can trade it any time on SimpleDEX.
What are the risks of holding GUARD?+
GUARD is a speculative meme token. Price is driven entirely by community demand and can rise or fall sharply. Never invest more than you can afford to lose. This is not financial advice.
Why does this site exist?+
To give newcomers a friendly, plain-English starting point for the XPR Network ecosystem — and to point them to the official sources for the full detail. It's a community resource, not an official one.

Meet Tim Rangel

The person behind GUARD and this site — a community builder, not a corporation.

Tim Rangel
@scubasteve4488

✕ Twitter / X ✦ Snipverse ✈ KSTO Telegram ⚔ GUARD Token

My name is Tim Rangel. I enjoy baseball and martial arts — that was my athletic history growing up. I'm a builder, creator, and active participant within the Metallicus ecosystem with a focus on infrastructure, accessibility, and community growth.

My background isn't in software engineering or traditional finance — I work in healthcare — but crypto pulled me in because of the technology behind it, not just the markets. Over time, that interest evolved into learning how identity, authentication, lending, payments, and digital ownership actually function beneath the surface.

That curiosity is what led me deeper into XPR Network, WebAuth Wallet, MetalX, LOAN Protocol, SimpleDEX, and the broader Metallicus ecosystem.

I created GUARD as a community-driven project inspired by the principles I believe matter most for the future of digital finance: ownership, security, accessibility, and education. GUARD is not an official representation of Metallicus — it is my way of contributing through community building, educational content, creativity, and ecosystem advocacy.

Why Metallicus?

Because I believe the next phase of crypto adoption depends less on speculation and more on infrastructure. What stands out to me about this ecosystem is its focus on solving real problems:

  • Simpler authentication through WebAuth
  • Modern approaches to digital identity and access
  • Decentralized lending and financial infrastructure
  • Payment systems and real-world integrations
  • Regulatory-minded growth and compliance
  • Better onboarding for everyday users
  • Building products designed for long-term usability
The Infrastructure Layer

I'm interested in the part of crypto that most people never see — the infrastructure layer. Whether I'm creating content, building the Metallicus Guardian project, participating in community spaces, or helping simplify technical concepts, my goal is the same:

Help make this technology easier to understand and more accessible to the people who will eventually use it every day.

About This Site

I built this site to give newcomers a friendly, plain-English starting point for the XPR Network ecosystem — and to showcase the communities, builders, and projects that make it special, alongside Metallicus and XPR Network themselves. It's an educational resource and a celebration of the people here, not an official product. If something's wrong or missing, reach out.
