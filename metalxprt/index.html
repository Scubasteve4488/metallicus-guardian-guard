MetalXPRt

Guard.IAN's guide to XPR Network and the Metallicus ecosystem, plugged into the AI you already use: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and other AI apps that let you add outside tools.

It gives your AI an index of the official sources and live tools to read the chain, so it looks facts up instead of guessing, and links every fact to its source.

The rules it follows

A source for every fact

With the source's own date. The live chain beats any document.

"I haven't verified that"

When nothing covers a question, it says so and names where to check.

No price talk

No predictions, and no telling anyone to buy, sell or hold.

No verdicts on people

It never calls anyone a scam. It reports what each source says.

Wallets aren't for targeting

It won't hand out who owns which wallet.

Safety first

Real outages and incidents are stated with their source. It never asks for a key or recovery phrase.
