What it can look up live

Is this the real token?

Checks a token against XPR's official token registry, the live chain, Metal X's market list and Alcor's labels, and reports what each source says.

Who am I about to pay?

The public record of one account in plain facts: what it is, how old it is, what can and can't be verified. A record, never a verdict.

Is something down?

The three official Metallicus status pages, read live and quoted in their own words and time stamps.

What's new since yesterday?

One dated list across status incidents, SimpleDEX launches and graduations, governance proposals, new agents and jobs, official releases and news.

What are the lending rates?

LOAN Protocol markets, rates and deposits, plus Metal X and Alcor market data, all read live.

What's happening on the other chains?

Metal Blockchain and its Bitcoin, Litecoin and Dogecoin chains, Metal L2, Ethereum and BNB Chain, plus XMD reserve reports and SEC filings.

Also: accounts, contracts, transactions and blocks, block producers, governance votes, NFTs, the XPR Agents directory, the testnet and the official help centre.

The rules it follows

A source for every fact

Each fact comes with its link and the source's own date. The live chain beats any document.

"I haven't verified that"

When nothing covers a question, it says so and names where to check, instead of filling the gap.

No price talk

No predictions, and no telling anyone to buy, sell or hold.

No verdicts on people

It never calls anyone a scam. It reports what each source says and what can't be verified.

Wallets aren't for targeting

It won't hand out who owns which wallet, so it can't become a tool for tracking or harassing people.

Safety first

Real outages and incidents are stated with their source. It never asks for a key or recovery phrase, and tells anyone who pastes one to move their funds.
