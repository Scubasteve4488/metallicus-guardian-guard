Service

Launch Rehearsal

Planned

Rehearse a launch before it is real, so the failure modes surface on a day when nothing is at stake.

What "Planned" means here

Planned means an approved direction with no completed implementation. There is no scope document, no deliverable list, no acceptance criteria, and no price — because none of those exist yet, not because they are being withheld.

It appears on the services page for one reason: the direction is real and it is better for you to see it labelled honestly than to find it announced later as though it had always been ready.

What would have to happen first

  • The three waitlist services complete timed dry-runs, so delivery cost is measured rather than guessed.
  • A rehearsal is run end to end at least once, on a rig or testnet, with the result written up.
  • Scope and exclusions are written from that run, not from an idea of it.
