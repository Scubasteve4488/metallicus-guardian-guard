Process

How a job would run

  1. You read the deliverables and exclusions.
  2. You supply exact identifiers — the account, token id, contract, transaction, collection or pool in question.
  3. StandGuard performs a free intake validation, only to confirm the request is in scope.
  4. If it is in scope, you purchase the matching XPR Agents service.
  5. The agent or operator accepts and starts the funded job.
  6. Work happens in a case workspace with a recorded source lock.
  7. The deliverable is checked against the stated acceptance criteria.
  8. Evidence is delivered through the XPR Agents job.
  9. You approve, request the one included revision, or use the platform dispute path.
  10. Any public use of your case requires separate explicit permission and redaction.

Revision and support boundary

  • One included revision, limited to correcting the agreed deliverable.
  • No change of subject after a job starts.
  • No unlimited chat or support.
  • If missing private project files block the work, you supply them or the work stops there.
  • Third-party downtime pauses the evidence claim rather than forcing a guess.
  • Refunds and disputes follow XPR Agents platform rules, not a private promise made on this site.
