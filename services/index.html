How a job would run

  1. You read what the job covers.
  2. You send the exact account, token or transaction.
  3. We check for free that it's something we can answer.
  4. If it is, you buy the job on XPR Agents.
  5. We do the work and deliver it there.
  6. You approve it, ask for the one included revision, or dispute it.

The limits

  • One revision included, to fix what we got wrong.
  • No swapping the question after we start.
  • No endless support.
  • Refunds follow the XPR Agents rules, not a private promise here.
