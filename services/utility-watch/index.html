Utility Watch

Planned

We watch the things your plan depends on, and tell you when one of them changes.

Only when it actually matters. Not a daily digest of noise.

What it can watch

  • Code repositories and releases.
  • Contracts and on-chain activity.
  • Tokens, pools, accounts, agents and collections.
  • Official docs and announcements.
  • Someone copying what you're doing.
  • Assumptions in your plan that stop being true.

What it won't do

  • Watch everything, forever, vaguely.
  • Send price alerts dressed up as strategy.
  • Collect private information.
  • Accuse anyone in public.
  • Fix anything on its own.
  • Replace a security audit or a lawyer.

What a report says

  1. What changed
  2. What's worth a look
  3. What we couldn't check
  4. What it does to your plan
  5. What we'd do about it
  6. When we look again

The shape of it

  • Thirty days.
  • A named project and an agreed list of things to watch.
  • Alerts straight away only for things you've said matter.
  • One summary at the end.
  • Private. Read-only. Buy it again if it was useful.
⚠
Planned, not running. It needs a baseline to compare against, and it needs to survive a change of session without losing track of what it was watching. Neither is proven yet.
