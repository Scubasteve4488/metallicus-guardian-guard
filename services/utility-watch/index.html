Utility Watch

Parked — not for sale

A follow-up to a Utility Forge report. Guard.IAN keeps an eye on the things your plan depends on, and tells you when one of them changes.

Only when it matters. Not a daily digest of noise.

It comes after the Forge. No price and no date yet.

What it can watch

  • Your token, pools, accounts and collections on XPR Network.
  • Contracts and on-chain activity your plan relies on.
  • Code repositories and releases.
  • Official docs and announcements.
  • Things your plan assumes that stop being true.

What it won't do

  • Watch everything, forever, vaguely.
  • Send price alerts dressed up as strategy.
  • Collect private information.
  • Accuse anyone in public.
  • Fix anything on its own.
  • Replace a security audit or a lawyer.

What an alert says

  1. What changed
  2. What it does to your plan
  3. What we'd do about it
  4. When we look again

The shape we have in mind

  • A set period, after a Forge report.
  • A named project and an agreed list of things to watch.
  • Alerts straight away only for things you've said matter.
  • One summary at the end.
  • Private and read-only.
⚠
Not for sale. It hasn't run on a real project yet, and it won't be listed until it has.
