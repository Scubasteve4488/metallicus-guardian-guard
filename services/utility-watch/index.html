Utility Watch

Planned

Utility Watch monitors a named project’s agreed sources and reports only the evidence that actually changes the plan. It begins only after a Forge or equivalent baseline exists — without a baseline there is nothing to detect a change against.

What it can watch

  • Official repositories and releases.
  • Contracts and public chain state.
  • Token, pool, account, agent, collection, dApp and endpoint changes.
  • Official documentation and product announcements.
  • Competitor duplication and ecosystem shifts.
  • Evidence that changes the selected utility plan.
  • Broken assumptions, dependencies, permissions or APIs.

What it refuses to do

  • Nonstop vague surveillance.
  • Price alerts dressed up as product strategy.
  • Private-data collection without permission.
  • Public accusations.
  • Autonomous corrective transactions.
  • Indefinite support for a single purchase.
  • Replacing a security audit or a legal review.

The report shape

  1. Verified
  2. Attention
  3. Could not verify
  4. Impact on plan
  5. Recommended action
  6. Sources and timestamps
  7. Next check

The intended first form: a 30-Day Utility Watch

  • A named project and exact subjects.
  • An agreed source list and check frequency.
  • Immediate alerts only for defined material events.
  • One final consolidated report.
  • Private by default, read-only, and bought again for another period only if it proved useful.
⚠
Planned, not running. It publishes only after durable baseline and state continuity are proven — that is, after a watch survives a change of chat, session or model without losing what it was watching. Chat history is not a state store.
