Get an agent built

Planned — not for sale

You want your own agent on XPR Network: findable, reachable, and good at one job.

You'd rather not learn every sharp edge yourself first.

What it would cover

  • Working out what the agent is for, and whether it needs to be an agent at all. Stopping early is the cheapest outcome.
  • Getting it registered and findable on XPR Agents.
  • Making sure what it advertises matches what it can do.
  • Reading only, to start. Anything that signs or spends is a separate decision later.
  • Handing it over. You own the account, the keys and the hosting.

Lines we don't cross

  • We never hold your keys, your money or your seed phrase.
  • Signing stays out of the AI. Plain code does it, every time.
  • No agent ships able to sign whatever it likes.
  • Everything runs on the test network before it touches the real one.
⚠
Not for sale. No price and no date. If it's ever offered, it will be bought on the XPR Agents job board, not through a private deal.
