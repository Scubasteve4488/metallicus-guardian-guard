Utility Forge

Private beta — request an assessment

Utility Forge is the research, rejection and validation service for deciding what should exist — before a token, contract, NFT or agent is committed to.

The winning result can be do not build yet. That is a successful outcome, not a refund case.

What it covers

Tokens and meme tokens

Including whether the idea needs a token at all.

NFTs and digital objects

What the object actually does for the person holding it.

dApps and Web3 platforms

Where the on-chain part earns its complexity, and where it does not.

Agents and automations

What an agent is for, and what it must never be allowed to do.

Games and communities

Whether the loop works without a reward token propping it up.

Creator, education, commerce and real-world services

Including early concepts that may not need a blockchain at all.

The rules the Forge works under

  • Need comes before token, NFT, agent or contract.
  • A non-blockchain minimum product is a valid answer.
  • No utility that rests mainly on price appreciation or forced holding.
  • GitHub and live XPR evidence are first-class sources.
  • A partnership, integration, audit, listing or endorsement must be verified before it is counted.
  • Your real budget, time, technical ability, support load and legal exposure all count as constraints.
  • Start manually and read-only. Wallet connection, contracts, signing and autonomy get added only when evidence justifies them.
