Launch Readiness Check

Waitlist

You're about to launch a token, NFT drop, app, agent or community project.

You want to know what's missing before everyone else finds out.

What you send

  • What you're launching.
  • Your website and code links.
  • The accounts, contracts and token IDs involved.
  • Who it's for, and what you're promising them.
  • Your launch date, if you have one.

What you get back

  • A snapshot of where the project really is.
  • Every public promise, lined up against the evidence.
  • A walk through what a new user actually experiences.
  • Gaps and contradictions.
  • What would block a launch, kept separate from nice-to-haves.
  • A fix list you could work through in a week.

What it is not

  • Not a code or contract audit.
  • Not legal advice.
  • Not a stamp of approval.
  • No promise your launch goes well.
  • Paying does not buy a kinder verdict.

When it counts as done

  • Every promise is marked: backed up, unsupported, unknown, or contradicted.
  • Exact accounts and code versions are written down.
  • Blockers are separated from improvements.
  • We invented no partnerships you don't have.
  • You know the next thing to do.
