Launch Readiness Check

Parked — not for sale

You're about to launch a token, NFT drop, app, agent or community project.

You want to know what's missing before everyone else finds out.

What you'd send

  • What you're launching.
  • Your website and code links.
  • The accounts, contracts and token IDs involved.
  • Who it's for, and what you're promising them.
  • Your launch date, if you have one.

What you'd get back

  • Where the project stands today, from its public record.
  • Your public promises next to what the chain and your links show.
  • What a new user actually runs into.
  • What would block a launch, kept apart from nice-to-haves.
  • A fix list you could work through in a week.

What it is not

  • Not a code or contract audit.
  • Not financial or legal advice.
  • Not a stamp of approval.
  • No promise your launch goes well.
  • Paying doesn't change what the check says.
⚠
Not for sale. No price and no date. Only the Utility Forge report is on the job board today.
