Agent Diagnostic

Planned — not for sale

You've built an agent on XPR Network. You're not sure it actually works from the outside.

The idea: check it the way a stranger would, and tell you what's broken.

What you'd send

  • The agent's account name and address.
  • Its code, if that's public.
  • What you meant it to do.
  • Whether it's on the test network or the real one.

What you'd get back

  • Whether people can find it on the XPR Agents registry.
  • Whether its published details are right.
  • Whether it answers when something contacts it.
  • Whether it could take a job today.
  • What it says it does, next to what it does.
  • A repair list, worst first.

What it is not

  • We never touch a private key. Don't send one.
  • Working from the outside says nothing about how secure the inside is.
  • Nothing is changed on your live system.
  • No code repairs.
  • No promise your agent wins work.
⚠
Not for sale. No price and no date. Only the Utility Forge report is on the job board today.
