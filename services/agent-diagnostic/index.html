Agent Diagnostic

Waitlist

You've built an agent on XPR Network. You're not sure it actually works from the outside.

We check it the way a stranger would, and tell you what's broken.

What you send

  • The agent's account name and address.
  • Its code, if that's public.
  • What you meant it to do.
  • Whether it's on the test network or the real one.

What you get back

  • Whether people can find it at all.
  • Whether its published details are correct.
  • Whether it answers when something contacts it.
  • Whether it could really take a job today.
  • What it claims to do, next to what it does.
  • Questions about who can sign for it.
  • A repair list, worst first.

What it is not

  • We never touch a private key. Don't send one.
  • A clean outside does not mean a secure inside, and we won't pretend otherwise.
  • We change nothing on your live system.
  • No code repairs unless you buy those separately.
  • No promise your agent wins work.

When it counts as done

  • We reached the agent and read its details.
  • We reproduced how it behaves.
  • Every untested claim is flagged as untested.
  • The first repair step is small and specific.
ℹ
We intend to run this on Guard.IAN itself and publish the result, including whatever it fails. That report is planned, not written.
