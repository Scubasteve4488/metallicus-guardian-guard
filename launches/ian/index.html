What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 403646618, on 15 September 2026, built by the producer catsvote.
  • 5 buys landed in that block.
  • 1 of our 5 wallets was in it, at place 5 of 5.
  • Our wallets also bought later: 5 wallets made 9 buys, 9,000 XPR in total, 33 s to 5 min 3 s after the first legal block (blocks 403646683 to 403647224).
  • Not all 5 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 4 buys in the first legal block came from 4 accounts, 20,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1883), 27 min 31 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by safeguard5@active and guardops@active (our wallets).
  • IAN was the first launch, and real money: the one wallet that made the first legal block (guardgrowth) bought 1,000 XPR.
