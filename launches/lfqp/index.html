What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 404834734, on 22 September 2026, built by the producer blocksforge.
  • 11 buys landed in that block.
  • 5 of our 5 wallets were in it, at places 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9 of 11.
  • All 5 were in the fastest 10, so this launch passes our test.
  • The other 6 buys in the first legal block came from 6 accounts, 30,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1885), 10 min 49 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by leafquip@active.
  • Test size: 1 XPR per wallet.
