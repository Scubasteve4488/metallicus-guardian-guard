What this counts and what it doesn't. Only StandGuard's own wallets are named as buyers; every other buy is shown as another buyer. The order is the order the chain's history reader returns the block's transactions, which is the producer's packing order. A pass or a fail says how fast the wallets were, not whether the token is any good.

What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 408086182, on 11 October 2026, built by the producer storexbp.
  • 10 buys landed in that block.
  • 7 of our 7 wallets were in it, at places 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 of 10.
  • All 7 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch passes our test.
  • The other 3 buys in the first legal block came from 3 accounts, 10,100 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1979), 36 min 45 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by safeguard5@guard (one of our wallets).
  • Test size: 0.01 XPR per wallet.
