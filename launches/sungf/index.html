What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 405160063, on 24 September 2026, built by the producer brotonbp.
  • 7 buys landed in that block.
  • None of our wallets bought this token, so this launch is not counted in our score.
  • The other 7 buys in the first legal block came from 7 accounts, 35,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1887), 9 min 33 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by stackhash@active.
  • Our fleet was not running: the old code was retired on 24 September and the rebuilt watcher started after this launch. This launch is not counted in our score.
