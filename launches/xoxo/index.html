What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 406065969, stamped 23:07:22 (chain time, UTC) and built by the producer snipverse.
  • 13 buys landed in that block.
  • 7 of our 7 wallets were in it, at places 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 of 13.
  • Not all 7 were in the fastest 10, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 6 buys came from 6 accounts, 30,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1890), 701 min 28 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was signed by safeguard5, one of our wallets.
  • Test size: 0.01 XPR per wallet. First launch on the new sender timing.
