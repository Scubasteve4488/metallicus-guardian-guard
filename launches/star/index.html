What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 405537350, on 26 September 2026, built by the producer cryptolions.
  • 9 buys landed in that block.
  • 1 of our 7 wallets was in it, at place 9 of 9.
  • Our wallets also bought later: 6 wallets made 6 buys, 0.06 XPR in total, 1 s after the first legal block (block 405537351).
  • Not all 7 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 8 buys in the first legal block came from 8 accounts, 40,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1888), 10 min 21 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by midsummer@active.
  • Test size: 0.01 XPR per wallet.
