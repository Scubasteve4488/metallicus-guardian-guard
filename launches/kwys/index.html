What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 406341837, on 1 October 2026, built by the producer madblocks.
  • 13 buys landed in that block.
  • 7 of our 7 wallets were in it, at places 1, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10 and 11 of 13.
  • Not all 7 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 6 buys in the first legal block came from 6 accounts, 30,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1891), 5 min 21 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by safeguard5@guard (one of our wallets).
  • Test size: 0.01 XPR per wallet.
