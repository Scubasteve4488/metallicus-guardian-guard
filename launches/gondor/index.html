What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 404310116, on 19 September 2026, built by the producer cafe.
  • 12 buys landed in that block.
  • 5 of our 5 wallets were in it, at places 4, 8, 10, 11 and 12 of 12.
  • Not all 5 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 7 buys in the first legal block came from 7 accounts, 35,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1884), 4 h 41 min after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by safeguard5@guard and guardgrowth@active (our wallets).
  • Test size: 1 XPR per wallet.
