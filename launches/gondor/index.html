What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 404310116, stamped 19:13:23 (chain time, UTC) and built by the producer cafe.
  • 12 buys landed in that block.
  • 5 of our 5 wallets were in it, at places 4, 8, 10, 11 and 12 of 12.
  • Not all 5 were in the fastest 10, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 7 buys came from 7 accounts, 35,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1884), 281 min 29 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was signed by safeguard5, one of our wallets.
  • Test size: 1 XPR per wallet.
