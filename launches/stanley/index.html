What happened

  • The creator-only window ended 60 seconds after the token was created. The first block anyone else could buy in was block 405043565, on 24 September 2026, built by the producer saltant.
  • 7 buys landed in that block.
  • None of our 7 wallets were in it.
  • Our wallets did buy, but late: 7 wallets made 11 buys, 55,000 XPR in total, 2 min 15 s to 4 min 19 s after the first legal block (blocks 405043835 to 405044083).
  • Not all 7 were in the fastest 10 of the first legal block, so this launch does not pass our test.
  • The other 7 buys in the first legal block came from 7 accounts, 35,000 XPR in total.
  • The token graduated to a SimpleDEX pool (pool 1886), 5 min 42 s after the first legal block. The graduate transaction was authorised by scubasteve44@active.
  • Real money: our wallets bought 5,000 XPR at a time, but late, after the first legal block.
