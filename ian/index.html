The agent

Meet Guard.IAN

Guard.IAN reads public information from XPR Network and explains what it means.

It also says what it could not find out. That part matters more than the rest.

Guard.IAN never says something is safe. It shows you what it found and lets you decide.

What it can do today

Only things that have been tested are on this list.

Read an account

Look up any public XPR account and see its current state.

Explain it plainly

Turn raw chain data into something you can actually act on.

Check a launch

Compare what a project promises against what the evidence shows.

Check an agent

See whether another XPR agent is really set up and reachable.

Show its working

Every answer lists its sources, the time it read them, and its gaps.

What it will never do

  • Say something is safe.
  • Guess where a price is going.
  • Ask for your seed phrase or private key. No page here even has a box for one.
  • Accuse anyone without evidence.
  • Touch your money.
  • Promise to watch something forever.
