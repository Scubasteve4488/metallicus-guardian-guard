The agent

Meet Guard.IAN

Guard.IAN is the evidence and service agent behind StandGuard. Ian reads public XPR information, explains what it means, states what is missing, and produces reviewable deliverables.

GUARD never says safe — GUARD gives clarity. Before you sign, let me look.

Capability

What Ian can do now

Only tested capabilities appear in this list. Anything untested sits under Testing or Planned on the status page instead.

Inspect public XPR account information

Reads current on-chain state for a named public account from public nodes.

Organize public evidence into a plain-English answer

Turns raw chain and indexer reads into something a non-specialist can act on.

Perform a bounded launch readiness review

Compares what a project promises publicly against what the evidence supports.

Diagnose an XPR agent’s public surface

Registration, endpoint, agent card, and service readiness, from public information only.

Produce a source-and-limitations receipt

Every answer carries its sources, retrieval times, and what it could not establish.

Limits

What Ian will not do

  • Declare something safe.
  • Predict token prices.
  • Accept seed phrases or private keys. Guard.IAN never asks for one, and no page on this site has a field for one.
  • Make a public accusation without adequate evidence.
  • Sign or move customer funds as part of a research service.
  • Promise uninterrupted monitoring.
  • Claim autonomous job completion until the job runner proves it.

Chat

Asking Guard.IAN a question

⚠
The chat is not wired into this page yet. The public chat door lives on the agent runtime, and this rebuilt site does not yet call it. Embedding a box that silently does nothing would be worse than saying so.

When the chat is connected here, it will state, on every answer: whether live chain or API data was used; the retrieval time; any source that failed; whether the answer is a quick public response or a paid-service deliverable; and that chat output is not automatically an XPR Agents job delivery.

In the meantime, the read-only account check runs entirely in your browser and answers the most common question without any of that machinery.

What these numbers do and do not mean

A trust score and a KYC level describe registry state. Neither is evidence that an agent — this one included — is honest, competent, or correct. Guard.IAN does not treat them as proof when reviewing anyone else’s agent, and you should not treat them as proof here.

The /health endpoint did not return machine-readable health at the time of the record. That is a known gap, tracked on the status page, and it belongs to the agent runtime rather than this website.

Report shape

Every answer is built the same way

The last two sections are the product. Any wallet tool can give you a green check; the disciplined statement of what could not be established is what is actually worth reading.

  1. Verified public facts
  2. Plain-English meaning
  3. Attention items
  4. Could not verify
  5. What would confirm it
  6. Sources and timestamps
  7. Scope and limitations
